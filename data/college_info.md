## Quick Guide for New Visitors
ABC Institute of Technology is an engineering and management college in Hyderabad, Telangana. It offers B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA and MCA programs, has hostels for boys and girls, college buses on 8 routes, a central library, sports facilities, a medical centre and an active placement cell. You can ask me about admissions, fees, hostel, bus routes, library, placements, exams, clubs, scholarships and contact details.

## About the College
ABC Institute of Technology was established in 1998 by the ABC Education Society. It is approved by AICTE, affiliated to the state technical university and accredited by NAAC with an A grade. Several B.Tech programs are accredited by NBA. The college has over 4,000 students, 280 faculty members and 40 acres of green campus.

## Vision and Mission
The vision of the college is to be a centre of excellence in technical education, research and innovation. The mission is to provide quality education, industry-ready skills, strong ethics and entrepreneurship opportunities to every student.

## Campus and Location
The campus is located on Main Road, Hyderabad, Telangana, about 25 km from the city centre. It includes academic blocks, workshops, laboratories, an auditorium with 800 seats, a seminar hall, an open-air theatre, hostels, a canteen, a sports complex, a medical centre and a bank with an ATM. The nearest metro station is 6 km away and the nearest railway station is 14 km away.

## How to Reach the College
By bus: TSRTC city buses stop at the college gate and the college runs its own buses on 8 routes. By metro: get down at the nearest metro station and take an auto for about 15 minutes. By train: the nearest railway station is 14 km away and cabs are available. By air: Rajiv Gandhi International Airport is about 35 km from the campus.

## Courses Offered Undergraduate
B.Tech programs: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) with 180 seats, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI&DS) with 120 seats, Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) with 120 seats, Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) with 60 seats, Mechanical Engineering with 60 seats, and Civil Engineering with 60 seats. The duration of B.Tech is 4 years with 8 semesters.

## Courses Offered Postgraduate
M.Tech programs in CSE, VLSI Design and Power Systems with 18 seats each and a duration of 2 years. MBA with 120 seats and a duration of 2 years with specializations in Finance, Marketing, HR and Business Analytics. MCA with 60 seats and a duration of 2 years.

## Departments and Labs
The college has departments of CSE, AI&DS, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, Management Studies, Computer Applications, and Humanities and Sciences. Labs include programming labs, AI and machine learning lab, IoT lab, robotics lab, VLSI lab, embedded systems lab, power systems lab, CAD and CAM lab, structural engineering lab, and a language lab.

## Admission Process
B.Tech admissions are through the state entrance exam (EAMCET) rank and JEE Main score, with counselling conducted by the state government. Management quota seats are filled on merit by the college. M.Tech admission is through GATE or PGECET. MBA admission is through ICET or CAT. MCA admission is through ICET. Lateral entry to the second year is through ECET for diploma holders.

## Eligibility Criteria
For B.Tech a student must have passed 12th with Maths, Physics and Chemistry with at least 45 percent marks (40 percent for reserved categories). For M.Tech a relevant B.Tech or BE degree is required. For MBA and MCA any bachelor degree with 50 percent marks is required, and MCA also requires Maths at 12th or degree level.

## Documents Required for Admission
Bring original and photocopies of 10th and 12th marksheets, entrance exam rank card, hall ticket, transfer certificate, study certificates from class 6 to 12, caste certificate if applicable, income certificate for scholarships, Aadhaar card, 6 passport size photographs, and a migration certificate if from another state board.

## Important Dates
Applications open in May every year on the college website. Counselling happens in July. Classes for first year B.Tech begin in the first week of September. The first semester exams are in December and the second semester exams are in May. Summer vacation runs from mid-May to the end of June.

## Fee Structure B.Tech
The B.Tech tuition fee is Rs 95,000 per year. The one-time admission fee is Rs 10,000. The caution deposit of Rs 5,000 is refundable at the end of the course. The university and exam fee is about Rs 4,000 per year. Management quota B.Tech students pay Rs 1,40,000 per year as tuition.

## Fee Structure PG Programs
The M.Tech tuition fee is Rs 70,000 per year. The MBA tuition fee is Rs 80,000 per year. The MCA tuition fee is Rs 65,000 per year. The one-time admission fee for PG courses is Rs 8,000 and the refundable caution deposit is Rs 5,000.

## Hostel Fees
The hostel fee including mess is Rs 60,000 per year for a three-sharing room, Rs 75,000 per year for a two-sharing room and Rs 1,00,000 per year for a single room with attached bathroom. A refundable hostel deposit of Rs 5,000 is collected at joining. Electricity is included for standard use.

## Other Fees
Bus transport fee is Rs 15,000 to Rs 25,000 per year depending on the route. The library fee is Rs 1,500 per year. The sports and cultural fee is Rs 2,000 per year. The ID card replacement fee is Rs 200. The attendance condonation fee is Rs 500 per subject. The re-evaluation fee is Rs 1,000 per paper.

## Fee Payment and Refund Policy
Fees can be paid online through the college portal using UPI, net banking, credit or debit card, or by demand draft in favour of ABC Institute of Technology. Tuition fee is paid in two installments, in September and in February. A late fine of Rs 100 per day applies after the due date. If a student withdraws before the classes start, the full fee except Rs 5,000 is refunded. No refund is given after one month of joining.

## Scholarships
Government scholarships are available for SC, ST, BC, EBC and minority students through the state scholarship portal and need an income certificate and caste certificate. The college gives merit scholarships: students scoring above 90 percent in 12th get a 50 percent tuition waiver, and those above 85 percent get a 25 percent waiver in the first year. Sports quota students can get up to 25 percent fee concession.

## Education Loans
The college has tie-ups with SBI, Canara Bank and HDFC Credila for education loans. A loan help desk in the administrative block guides students with documents such as the admission letter, fee structure, income proof and guarantor details. Loans up to Rs 10 lakh are available without collateral.

## Hostel Facilities for Boys
The boys hostel has 4 blocks with rooms for 1200 students. Facilities include high speed Wi-Fi, 24x7 water and power backup, a reading room, a TV and recreation room, indoor games, a laundry service, a gym, a hostel canteen and a water purifier on every floor. The warden stays on campus and CCTV cameras cover the common areas.

## Hostel Facilities for Girls
The girls hostel has 3 blocks with rooms for 800 students and is inside the campus with restricted entry. It has lady wardens, 24x7 security guards, CCTV, Wi-Fi, a reading room, a gym, a laundry service, a power backup and a separate dining hall. A visitors room is available for parents.

## Hostel Rules and Timings
Hostel in-time is 9:30 PM for first year students and 10:00 PM for other years. Late entry needs prior permission from the warden. Visitors are allowed only in the visitors room from 4 PM to 6 PM on Sundays. Ragging, smoking, alcohol and electric heaters are strictly prohibited. Students must carry their hostel ID card at all times.

## Mess and Food in Hostel
The mess serves breakfast from 7:30 to 8:30 AM, lunch from 12:30 to 2:00 PM, snacks from 5:00 to 5:45 PM and dinner from 7:30 to 9:00 PM. Both vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals are available. Non-vegetarian food is served 3 days a week. A mess committee of students checks food quality every month.

## Bus Rules and Booking
To get a bus seat, fill the transport form at the transport office with your address and choose the nearest route. Pay the fee online or at the accounts office. Bus passes are issued within 3 days. Students may change routes once per semester with permission. Day scholars can also park two-wheelers on campus with a parking sticker.

## Library
The central library is open from 8 AM to 8 PM on weekdays and 9 AM to 2 PM on Saturdays and is closed on Sundays. It has over 50,000 books, 200 journals and access to IEEE, Springer, Elsevier and NPTEL digital libraries. Students can borrow 4 books for 14 days and the late fine is Rs 2 per day. A digital library with 60 computers and a quiet reading hall with 300 seats are available.

## Computer Centre and Wi-Fi
The campus has Wi-Fi coverage in all academic blocks, the library and hostels with a 1 Gbps internet connection. The computer centre has over 500 systems open from 8 AM to 6 PM. Students get a personal login for Wi-Fi. Licensed software such as MATLAB, AutoCAD and Microsoft tools is available in the labs.

## Canteen and Food Court
The campus has a main canteen and a food court serving breakfast, meals, snacks, juices and tea. The canteen is open from 8 AM to 5 PM. Meals cost around Rs 60 to Rs 100. There is also a coffee shop near the library and a fruit juice stall near the sports complex.

## Sports and Gym
The sports complex has a cricket ground, football field, volleyball, basketball and tennis courts, a badminton indoor stadium, table tennis, chess and carrom. A well-equipped gym is open from 6 AM to 8 AM and 4:30 PM to 7 PM. The college has a physical director and coaches. The annual Sports Day is held in January and teams take part in inter-college tournaments.

## Medical Facilities
A medical centre inside the campus is open from 9 AM to 5 PM with a qualified doctor and nurses. A 24 hour ambulance is available for emergencies. The college has tie-ups with a nearby hospital 3 km away. Basic medicines and first aid are free. All students get health insurance coverage of Rs 1 lakh.

## Bank ATM and Other Facilities
A bank branch with an ATM is on campus and is open on working days from 10 AM to 4 PM. There is a stationery and Xerox shop, a bookstore selling textbooks and lab records, a post box and a general store. A free drinking water facility is available in every block.

## Placements Overview
The placement cell conducts campus drives from August to March. Last year the highest package was 24 LPA, the average package was 5.2 LPA, and the placement rate was 87 percent. More than 150 companies visited the campus. Top recruiters include TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Cognizant, Amazon, Capgemini, Deloitte and Tech Mahindra.

## Placement Training and Internships
Training in aptitude, soft skills, coding and mock interviews starts from the second year. Students are encouraged to complete internships of 4 to 8 weeks in summer. The college has MoUs with companies like Infosys Springboard, Microsoft and Cisco for certifications and internships. Students are eligible to sit for placements if they have no backlogs and 60 percent marks.

## Higher Studies and Entrepreneurship
The college guides students for GATE, GRE, CAT and IELTS with coaching classes inside the campus. The Entrepreneurship Cell and Incubation Centre support student startups with mentoring, workspace and seed funding up to Rs 2 lakh.

## Examinations and Grading
Every semester has two internal exams and one end-semester exam. Internal marks carry 30 percent weightage and external exam marks carry 70 percent. A student must score at least 40 percent in the end exam to pass. Grading uses a 10 point CGPA system. Results are announced within 30 days of the exam. Supplementary exams are held twice a year.

## Attendance Rules
A minimum of 75 percent attendance is required to appear for the semester exams. Students with 65 to 74 percent attendance can apply for condonation with a medical certificate and a fee of Rs 500 per subject. Students below 65 percent attendance are detained and must repeat the semester. Attendance is updated weekly on the student portal and parents get an SMS.

## Academic Calendar and Class Timings
Classes run from Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM with a lunch break from 12:30 to 1:20 PM. Each day has 7 periods of 50 minutes. The second and fourth Saturdays are holidays. The college follows the state government holiday list.

## Clubs and Student Activities
Student clubs include the Coding Club, Robotics Club, AI Club, Cultural Club, Photography Club, Literary Club, Entrepreneurship Cell, Music and Dance Club, and Social Service Club. Students can join the NSS and NCC units. Clubs are open for registration in the first month of every year.

## Annual Events and Festivals
Techfest is held every February with coding contests, robotics competitions and paper presentations. The Cultural Fest is held in March with music, dance and drama. Sports Day is in January. Freshers Day is in October and Graduation Day is in June. A hackathon is organised every semester with cash prizes.

## Safety Anti-Ragging and Grievances
The campus is a ragging-free zone and has an Anti-Ragging Committee and helpline. Ragging leads to suspension and legal action. A Women Safety Cell and an Internal Complaints Committee handle harassment complaints. Students can submit grievances using the suggestion boxes, the college portal or by meeting the Dean of Student Affairs. CCTV and security guards cover the campus 24x7.

## Rules Dress Code and Identity Card
Students must wear the college ID card at all times on campus. Formal or decent casual dress is required, and on Wednesdays students wear the college uniform T-shirt. Mobile phones are allowed but must be silent in classrooms and labs. Smoking and drugs are strictly prohibited on campus.

## Research and Faculty
The college has 280 faculty members, of which 90 hold a PhD. Faculty are active in research with funded projects from AICTE and DST. The Centre for Research and Innovation supports students in publishing papers and filing patents. Each class has a mentor to guide students on academics and personal issues.

## Alumni and Industry Links
The alumni association has over 15,000 members working across the world. The annual alumni meet is held in December. The college has MoUs with more than 40 industries and universities for guest lectures, workshops and internships.
